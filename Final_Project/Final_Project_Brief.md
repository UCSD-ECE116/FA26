# Final project

The final project culminates ECE116 by connecting sensing, local classification, and a justified
decision to act in an application you choose. It contributes 25 percent of the course grade.

Read the [approved handout](Final_Project_Handout.md) for the complete requirements and rubric,
or [download its PDF](Final_Project_Handout.pdf). Use the
[generic two-column Word template](Final_Project_Report_Template.docx) for formatting; you choose
the report's organization. [Optional direction modules](Project_Directions.md) offer ideas.

The classifier distinguishes at least two target classes plus other/background. Submit three
files together through Canvas: a PDF with four technical pages plus one AI-use page and one
reference page, a 3-5 minute MP4 demonstration, and a supporting trial-data ZIP. The report and
data explain the analysis; the video shows the functioning system. There is one combined grade,
with 10 points for model-failure analysis and a proposed mitigation. No additional implemented
version or proposal is required.

Due December 4, 2026, at 7 p.m. Pacific; the course grace/late policy and December 7 hard close
apply. The handout is available from the beginning of the quarter. An office-hour discussion
with Professor Wang about your idea is strongly encouraged and optional.
